Dynasty Room

Privacy policy

Last updated September 29, 2026. Dynasty Room (“we”) is operated from Monterrey, Nuevo León, Mexico. Contact: support@dynasty-room.com.

What we collect

  • Your Google account email and a user ID, when you sign in with Google. We don’t receive your Google password or access your Gmail, Drive or contacts.
  • Your Sleeper username and Sleeper user ID, which you type in, and the league you choose. Everything else about your leagues (rosters, scores, projections, transactions) is public data we read from Sleeper each time you visit; we don’t need or ask for your Sleeper password.
  • Play-money bets you place in the sportsbook, so your league’s leaderboard can show them. Other signed-in users can see bets and Sleeper usernames on leaderboards.
  • Subscription status. Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number; we keep your Stripe customer ID and whether your subscription is active.
  • Gazette editions written for you by AI, stored so we don’t have to write them twice.
  • Settings stored in your browser (local storage and IndexedDB): your selected league, language, odds format and a cached copy of Sleeper’s player list. We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

Only to run Dynasty Room: to sign you in, show your leagues, save your bets and settings, unlock Premium features, and write your gazette. We don’t sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase: accounts and database.
  • Stripe: payments and billing.
  • Anthropic: writes AI gazette editions. It receives that week’s league results (team names, manager usernames, player names and scores), not your email or payment details.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the website.
  • Sleeper and ESPN’s public scoreboard: your browser requests public league and game data from them directly.

These providers may store data outside Mexico, including in the United States.

Your rights

Under Mexico’s Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (LFPDPPP) you can access, correct, cancel or object to the use of your personal data (ARCO rights). Email support@dynasty-room.com from the address you sign in with, and we’ll respond within 20 business days. You can change your Sleeper link and clear your bets from your account page, and cancel your subscription from the billing portal at any time. When you ask us to delete your account we delete your profile, bets and gazettes; Stripe keeps billing records as the law requires.

Children

Dynasty Room is not for children under 13, and the sportsbook is intended for adults. The sportsbook uses play money only.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we’ll post the new version here with a new date and tell signed-in users in the app.
